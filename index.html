Meet Our Pastor

Rev. Eric Owusu Ansah

Rev. Eric Owusu Ansah is a devoted servant of God with over 20 years of pastoral ministry and church planting experience. Eight years ago, God led him to establish Praise Sanctuary Assemblies of God, where he continues to shepherd God's people with passion and wisdom.

Throughout his ministry journey, Rev. Ansah has served alongside spiritual giants including Rev. Dr. Kwadwo Bempah (his father in the Lord), Pastor Mensah Otabil, Bishop Charles Agyin Asare, and Bishop Dag Heward Mills. His heart burns with one mission: to see people encounter God's power and live transformed lives.

Our Ministries

Youth Ministry

Empowering the next generation with biblical truth, leadership development, and vibrant fellowship. Our youth discover their purpose and passion for God in a dynamic, engaging environment.

Men's Fellowship

Building strong men of God who lead their families with integrity, support each other through life's challenges, and stand firm in their faith as warriors for the Kingdom.

Women's Fellowship

Creating a safe space where women can grow spiritually, support one another, discover their God-given gifts, and make a powerful impact in their families and communities.

Young Singles (YS)

A vibrant community for young single adults to build meaningful relationships, navigate life's transitions with godly wisdom, and prepare for their future with purpose and faith.

Children's Ministry

Nurturing young hearts with Bible stories, creative worship, and age-appropriate teaching that lays a strong foundation of faith and helps children fall in love with Jesus.

Administration

The backbone of our church operations, ensuring smooth coordination of events, financial stewardship, facility management, and supporting all ministries to function effectively in God's kingdom work.
