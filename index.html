A Word of Welcome & Hope

From Our Lead Pastor

Beloved in Christ,

Welcome to Praise Sanctuary Assemblies of God—a place where every heart finds hope and every voice joins in giving God His praise!

Whether you are young or old, weary or joyful, broken or whole, there is a place for you here. We believe that God deserves praise in a very different way—authentic, passionate, and transformative. Our doors are open, our arms are wide, and our hearts are ready to embrace you.

In a world filled with uncertainties, we stand as a beacon of hope, reminding you that God's love never fails. Here, we don't just attend church; we become family. We don't just hear the Word; we live it out together.

I personally invite you to join us as we worship, grow, and make a difference in our community and beyond. Come as you are, and discover the hope that only Jesus Christ can give.

With love and blessings,
Rev. Eric Owusu Ansah
Lead Pastor

About Praise Sanctuary AG

Our Story, Mission & Leadership

Our Story

Praise Sanctuary Assemblies of God was born from a divine vision to create a place where authentic worship meets transformative hope. Located in Ejisu-Kwamo, Israel, we are a community committed to giving God His praise in a very different way—with passion, authenticity, and excellence.

Under the leadership of Rev. Eric Owusu Ansah, our church has become a beacon of hope for both the young and old, providing spiritual guidance, community support, and a place where every person can encounter the living God.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To worship God with authenticity and excellence, transforming lives through the power of the Gospel while providing hope, healing, and purpose to every generation in our community and beyond.

👁️ Our Vision

To become a global church with the priority of winning souls and building disciples for Christ.

Our Leadership

Rev. Eric Owusu Ansah

Lead Pastor

Rev. Eric Owusu Ansah is the founding and lead pastor of Praise Sanctuary Assemblies of God. With a heart for worship and a passion for souls, he leads our congregation with wisdom, compassion, and unwavering dedication to God's Word.

His vision for the church is clear: to create a place where authentic worship transforms lives and where every person—young or old—finds hope, purpose, and a family in Christ.

Ministries & Departments

Find Your Place to Serve and Grow

Every member is a minister. Find where you fit and start making a difference today!

🌍 Evangelism & Missions

Spreading the Gospel locally and globally through outreach programs, mission trips, and community evangelism. We are passionate about reaching the lost and making disciples.

🙏 Prayer & Intercession

The spiritual engine of our church, dedicated to fervent prayer and intercession for the church, community, and world needs. Join us in seeking God's face.

⚙️ Administration

Managing church operations, finances, facilities, and ensuring smooth organizational functioning. Excellence in administration enables effective ministry.

📹 Media Ministry

Capturing and sharing God's work through photography, videography, live streaming, and social media ministry. Telling the story of what God is doing.

👶 Children Service

Nurturing young hearts with age-appropriate worship, Bible lessons, and activities that build strong faith foundations for the next generation.

📖 Sunday School & Discipleship

Teaching God's Word, discipleship training, and equipping believers for spiritual growth, maturity, and effective Christian living.

🎵 Choir & Music Ministry

Leading worship through music, choir performances, and creating an atmosphere of praise that ushers in God's presence.

🚪 Ushering

First point of contact, welcoming members and visitors, ensuring orderly services and creating a comfortable worship environment for all.

💫 Young Singles

Building strong faith foundations for young adults, providing fellowship, mentorship, and support for singles navigating life and relationships.
